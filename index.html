About Reevanti Global

Your reliable partner in eCommerce and international trade.

Reevanti Global is a dynamic eCommerce and international trading company committed to delivering quality products and dependable service across domestic and global markets.

We operate through leading online marketplaces such as Amazon and Flipkart, offering carefully sourced products across multiple categories including Home & Kitchen, Electronics, Health & Wellness, Sports & Fitness, Lifestyle Products, and Everyday Essentials.

Beyond eCommerce, Reevanti Global actively engages in export and import trading, connecting manufacturers, suppliers, and buyers across international markets. Our focus is on creating sustainable business relationships built on trust, transparency, and mutual growth.

Every product we sell and every partnership we establish reflects our commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

At Reevanti Global, we believe commerce is not just about transactions—it is about building long-term value and trust.
