About Reevanti Global

MSME-Registered eCommerce Brand — AI Digital Products & Indian Artisan Crafts

Reevanti Global is an MSME-registered eCommerce company based in Navi Mumbai, India, dedicated to delivering quality products to customers across India and internationally.

We specialise in two carefully curated categories — AI-powered eBooks & Digital Products including premium prompt guides, productivity resources, and knowledge tools for professionals, content creators, marketers, and entrepreneurs; and Handcrafted & Artisan products — sustainably made, eco-friendly goods crafted by skilled Indian artisans, available for domestic buyers and international traders.

As an exporter and international trader, we bridge India's rich artisan craft heritage with global markets — making authentic Indian handicrafts accessible worldwide. Our digital products leverage the power of Artificial Intelligence (AI) to help professionals work smarter, faster, and more effectively.

At Reevanti Global, we believe every customer deserves access to genuine, fairly priced products — whether it's an AI eBook that transforms your productivity, or a handcrafted piece that tells the story of India's artisan communities.
